# Creating an artist

Sometimes, you want to create an artist, for an alias or other use cases. Simply click on "Create artist"

![Capture D’écran 2026 01 13 À 15 49 27](/images/Captured’écran2026-01-13à15.49.27.png)

# How artists are created

When you accept a submission, we detect artists of the submissions, and we are creating them if they are not already existing.
