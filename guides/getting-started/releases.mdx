# Release list

You will find all your releases in the "releases" section of the app sidebar.

![Capture D’écran 2026 01 13 À 15 18 07](/images/Captured’écran2026-01-13à15.18.07.png)

Then you will have a list of all your release with different filters to filter them as you want (name, artists, labels, type etc.)

![Capture D’écran 2026 01 13 À 15 18 50](/images/Captured’écran2026-01-13à15.18.50.png)

On each release line you will find these 3 icons :

![Capture D’écran 2026 01 13 À 15 19 56](/images/Captured’écran2026-01-13à15.19.56.png)

The first icon will redirect you to the release page.

The second icon will downlaod all the assets presents on the release (cover, master etc...). Assets are empty at the beginning.

The third icon will cancel a release if needed.
