On Labelbase you can create as much playlists as you want. Playlist are very useful for commercial, and sync purpose. Each catalog can be private or public.

We are generating a page for each playlist, and an unique link that you can send to who ever you want.

# Playlists list

You can access your playlists by clicking on "playlists" item on the app sidebar.

![Capture D’écran 2026 01 13 À 15 39 46](/images/Captured’écran2026-01-13à15.39.46.png)

Then you will see all your playlists. Default, playlist list is empty.

![Capture D’écran 2026 01 13 À 15 39 53](/images/Captured’écran2026-01-13à15.39.53.png)

Each playlist item will display :

- cover (if uploaded)
- name
- number of tracks
- visibility (public/private)
- date of creation

and icons :

![Capture D’écran 2026 01 13 À 15 40 25](/images/Captured’écran2026-01-13à15.40.25.png)

The first icon copy the link of the playlist.

The second icon copy the access code of the playlist (if private)

The third icon delete the playlist

the last one open the playlist pannel.  (you can also click on the playlist title)

# Playlist pannel

By clicking on the playlist title you will have a pannel opening 

![Capture D’écran 2026 01 13 À 15 40 08](/images/Captured’écran2026-01-13à15.40.08.png)

Here you will find all tracks in the playlist. You can re-organize all your tracks order. You can also delete track by selecting them on ticking the checkboxes :

![Capture D’écran 2026 01 13 À 15 44 26](/images/Captured’écran2026-01-13à15.44.26.png)

And then clicking "Delete" on the bottom action bar.

# Uploading a playlist cover

You can upload a playlist cover by opening the playlist pannel, and clicking on the existing cover or placeholder and upload :
