On labelbase we provide you a storage to avoid using an external tools and switching between different tools. Depending on your plan you will have different sizes of storage.

# See my storage

You can find everything you storage in the "storages" section of the app sidebar.

![Capture D’écran 2026 01 13 À 15 54 57](/images/Captured’écran2026-01-13à15.54.57.png)

Then you will see the storage page.

![Capture D’écran 2026 01 13 À 15 54 52](/images/Captured’écran2026-01-13à15.54.52.png)

Here you will find all medias uploaded to labelbase (logos, avatars, covers, masters, stems ...)

## What if there are already some folders ?

It's completely normal. We are automatically creating folders for each labels created on labelbase. And then in each labels, we are creating folders for each releases.

# How to create my own folder ?

It's easy. On the storage page, click "Create Folder" on top.

![Capture D’écran 2026 01 13 À 15 55 02](/images/Captured’écran2026-01-13à15.55.02.png)

Name your folder, and **voilà** !
