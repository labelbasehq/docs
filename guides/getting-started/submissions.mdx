# Accept/Decline a submission

To accept or decline a submission, simply click on the status of the submission and choose the status you want to update to.

![Capture D’écran 2026 01 13 À 15 15 10](/images/Captured’écran2026-01-13à15.15.10.png)

If you choose "Feedback" a pop up will automatically be opened with a feedback requested.

# What happened when a submission is accepted ?

If you accept a submission, it will automatically : 

- Create a new release (you will find it on the release section of the app)
- Create an artist for each submission artists referred on the submission (if not already existing)
