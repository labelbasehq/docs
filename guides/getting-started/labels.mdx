Labelbase is workspace based. It means, a workspace can own multiple labels (based on your plan).

# List your labels

To get the list of your labels, simply click on the "labels" section of the app sidebar 

![Capture D’écran 2026 01 13 À 15 50 38](/images/Captured’écran2026-01-13à15.50.38.png)

And then you will have all you created labels 

![Capture D’écran 2026 01 13 À 15 50 44](/images/Captured’écran2026-01-13à15.50.44.png)

# How to create a label

To create a label simply click on "Create label" button on top of the labels page.

![Capture D’écran 2026 01 13 À 15 50 49](/images/Captured’écran2026-01-13à15.50.49.png)

<Info>
  The color will be used to emphasis your label name on releases and submissions. It's a visual indicator.
</Info>


# How to receive submission for a label ?

on labelbase, you have an "integrations" section. Each integration is linked to a label and not to a workspace. See the integrations section of the documentation.
