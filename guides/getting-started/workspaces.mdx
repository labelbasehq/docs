On labelbase, we are workspace based. It means, a workspace is a secured space for all your labels. It's like the big company managing all your labels.

# How many label can I create ?

As much as your plan handle.

# How to create a workspace

Simply click on your workspace logo : 

![Capture D’écran 2026 01 13 À 16 00 45](/images/Captured’écran2026-01-13à16.00.45.png)

Click "Create workspace" and follow instructions.

<Warning>
  Each workspace has it own billing system. We are billing on each workspace you are creating. Of course we have a free tier if needed.
</Warning>

# Updating my workspace

When you have selected a workspace, you click on "settings" item on the app sidebar.

![Capture D’écran 2026 01 13 À 16 02 02](/images/Captured’écran2026-01-13à16.02.02.png)

Here you will have 3 tabs

## Tab 1 - members

Here you can manage your workspace members. Based on the plan you choose you will have more or less members slots.

![Capture D’écran 2026 01 13 À 16 03 26](/images/Captured’écran2026-01-13à16.03.26.png)

## Tab 2  - billing informations

Everything related to billing and current usage regarding of your plan.

<Info>
  We are using stripe as payment provider.
</Info>

![Capture D’écran 2026 01 13 À 16 03 31](/images/Captured’écran2026-01-13à16.03.31.png)

## Tab 3 - Settings

Here you can manage some infos : 

- Upload a workspace logo
- Change the workspace name
- Delete the workspace
- Ask for your Data (GDPR)
- And activate some features
