If you want it, you can use our own public form to receive your releases.

Simply go to "integrations" items on the app sidebar 

![Capture D’écran 2026 01 13 À 16 12 27](/images/Captured’écran2026-01-13à16.12.27.png)

Choose the label you want

![Capture D’écran 2026 01 13 À 16 12 45](/images/Captured’écran2026-01-13à16.12.45.png)

And activate "Submission Form"

![Capture D’écran 2026 01 13 À 16 13 02](/images/Captured’écran2026-01-13à16.13.02.png)

# How to customize it ?

Simply click on "details" on the "submission form" card, and you can customize everything.

![Capture D’écran 2026 01 13 À 16 13 47](/images/Captured’écran2026-01-13à16.13.47.png)

<Warning>
  Don't forget to click on "save" on top to save your changes.
</Warning>


# Where do I find the shareable link ?

On the "Submission Form" integration card, click on the last icon to copy the link :

![Capture D’écran 2026 01 13 À 16 15 03](/images/Captured’écran2026-01-13à16.15.03.png)

Or click on the first one to open it.
