At labelbase, we are using AI (Artificial Intelligence) for audio metadata enrichement. \
For each track uploaded, our AI will analyze and extract different informations :

- BPM
- Key
- Mood
- Genre
- Instruments
- LUFS
- Vocals
- Energy
- Track metadata - Like duration, bit depth, bit rate, channels etc.

# Does my track is publicly uploaded to an AI ?

NO.

We are owning the server and we are working with https://essentia.upf.edu/ \
The track will not be sent to a public LLM, so you can easily sleep, nothing will be leaked.

# Why AI ?

In an evolving music indutry, it's important to get as much information as possible for sync, and ads. It helps you having a perfect enriched and browsable catalog, to help you having more sync/placement results.
